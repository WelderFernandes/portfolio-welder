# Design Rules

## Typography
- Use modern, highly legible sans-serif fonts (e.g., Inter, Roboto, or system fonts).
- Ensure proper visual hierarchy with clear distinctions between H1, H2, H3, and body text.
- Maintain adequate line-height, letter-spacing, and kerning for maximum readability.
- Avoid huge untracked typefaces.

## Colors
- Use a curated, harmonious color palette (e.g., HSL tailored colors).
- Ensure high contrast for accessibility.
- Avoid cliché combinations like purple fonts on dark backgrounds or glowing colored outlines.
- Provide clear hover and active states for interactive elements.

## Layout & Components
- Minimalist and function-driven design ("Less, but better"). Strip away unnecessary decorative elements.
- Fluidly responsive layout without excessive content shifting. Sub-components must be fluidly responsive to their container.
- Avoid over-nested cards (rounded cards containing three or more nested cards inside).
- Avoid grid backgrounds or particle mesh overlays unless specifically required for branding.
- Avoid bento boxes stuffed with unrelated icons.

## Aesthetics & Interactions
- Aim for a premium, state-of-the-art feel. Every pixel must earn its place.
- Use subtle micro-interactions and hover effects to make the interface feel alive.
- Add depth and texture to containers and visual elements; avoid completely flat/textureless surfaces unless intended by a specific design system.
